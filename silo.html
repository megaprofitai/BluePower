A controlled no-entry method for restoring silo flow

No-entry silo cleaning is a method used to restore flow without sending personnel into confined spaces. Bluepower uses BinDrill, BinWhip and, where relevant, Cardox to create access, loosen compacted material and restore flow without confined-space entry.

1Access

Create access into the compacted material without entry.

2Cleaning

Loosen and remove build-ups using controlled no-entry cleaning technology.

3Flow restoration

Restore stable material flow and full discharge performance.
