What Is Silo Buildup?

Silo buildup refers to material that adheres to internal surfaces and accumulates over time. In extreme cases, buildup can reduce capacity by 50–70%. The problem is particularly prevalent for powder materials but can occur with almost any bulk material.

Causes of Buildup

Moisture

Moisture is the single largest factor — condensation on cold silo walls creates a sticky layer.

Material properties

Material properties like fat content and natural binders contribute.

Electrostatic charge

Electrostatic charge attracts particles to metal walls.

Surface condition

Rough surfaces and corrosion provide anchor points.

Low movement zones

Zones with little movement accumulate more.

Why Buildup Is Dangerous

Reduced capacity

Reduced capacity affects all logistics.

Cross-contamination

Cross-contamination from old material can lead to recalls.

Structural overload

Structural overload can result from asymmetric loading.

Fire hazard

Organic materials can create a fire hazard.

Pests and microbial growth

Pests and microbial growth can develop in moist deposits.

Conventional Cleaning Methods

Manual scraping

Manual scraping requires dangerous silo entry.

High-pressure washing

High-pressure washing introduces water and requires drying.

Sandblasting

Sandblasting is abrasive and generates dust.

Chemical treatment

Chemical treatment creates waste handling problems.
