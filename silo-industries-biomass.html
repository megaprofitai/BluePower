Common Challenges in Energy & Biomass

Bridging — moist biomass forms stable arches over the outlet blocking all flow

Ratholing — fuel channels through a narrow opening while most material hangs in place

Self-heating and fire risk — biological degradation generates heat that can lead to spontaneous ignition

Condensation and clumping — temperature differences between inside and outside create condensation causing pellets to swell and stick together

Mould growth and biological contamination during humid standstill periods

Mechanical wear on extraction systems from compacted, stuck material

Uneven fuel quality to the combustion plant due to FIFO violations (first in, first out)

Why It Happens

Biomass blocks silos due to the material's natural properties:

Fibrous structure

Wood chips, bark and straw have irregular shapes that interlock and form stable bridges, even over wide outlets.

Hygroscopicity

Biomass actively absorbs moisture. Pellets can increase in volume by 30–50% when wetted, creating enormous pressure against silo walls.

Biological activity

Unlike minerals, biomass is organic and decomposes. This process generates heat (up to 70–80°C) and can in worst cases lead to smouldering fires that are extremely difficult to extinguish.

Compaction

Under its own pressure in tall silos, biomass compresses into an almost solid mass at the bottom layer. Extraction screws and feeders cannot break the material loose.

Seasonal variations

Winter operation with cold outdoor air and a warm, humid silo creates heavy condensation that worsens all the above problems.

Our Approach

Blue Power offers comprehensive solutions for the energy sector's silo challenges:

Gentle mechanical cleaning: We use methods that remove blockages without damaging the silo's internal lining or extraction systems. Especially important for silos with temperature monitoring and fire suppression systems.

Thermal mapping: When self-heating is suspected, we use thermal cameras to map hotspots before cleaning. Safety first — always.

Preventive flow aids: Installation of vibration plates, air cannons or slide coatings that reduce friction and prevent material build-up.

ATEX-certified operations: Biomass dust is explosive. All our equipment and procedures comply with the ATEX directive for work in explosive zones.

Maintenance agreements with seasonal focus: We plan inspections and cleaning in line with the plant's operational cycle — intensive maintenance before the heating season.
