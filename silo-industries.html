Industries

We serve facilities across multiple sectors, each with unique material and safety requirements.

Cement & Minerals

The cement industry presents some of the most demanding environments for silo storage. Fine dust, moisture and chemical reactions cause materials to harden and block silos.

Energy & Biomass

The energy sector depends on reliable fuel supply from silos and bunkers. Biomass creates unique challenges with self-ignition and bridging.

Feed & Grain

The feed and grain industry stores vast quantities of raw materials in silos. Product quality and food safety impose strict requirements on hygiene and cross-contamination.

Chemicals & Bulk Solids

The chemical industry and bulk solids handling impose the strictest requirements on silo cleaning — toxic, reactive, explosive or corrosive materials.
